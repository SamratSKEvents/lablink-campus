# LabLink — Verified Evidence Base

Checked against primary sources on 2026-11-23. Each figure describes its own study setting, not all universities.

## Supporting context

- Biopharma uses capital equipment at about 35% efficiency (McKinsey, as cited by Lab Manager). **Not verified against the McKinsey page**, so use it only as supporting context.
- Shared facilities already enforce no-show rules: Notre Dame cancels a booking after 8 minutes without check-in; UT Dallas charges for slots not cancelled 24 hours ahead; Penn restricts access for repeat no-shows. These justify LabLink's QR check-in and automatic slot release.
- China: under 50% of university instruments were shared in 2014, versus over 90% by 2022 (People's Daily, citing an official). Mandated sharing works at national scale.

## Do NOT use

- "31,000+ instruments / 65,000+ users, PIB 21 Sep 2026": the release could not be verified. Use row 9 instead.
- "Most lab instruments sit idle 60–70% of the time" and "22–27% utilisation" from vendor blogs (Capneteq, Scispot): no primary source.
